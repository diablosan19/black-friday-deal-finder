How we check a deal. Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from the source named on its card — The Verge, Gizmodo, Slickdeals, Tom's Guide, Benzinga, or a Walmart roundup — each naming the product, the store, and both prices. The discount shown is computed from those two prices, never from a claimed percentage. NEW LOW is used only when the source says the price is the lowest it has seen; STRONG DEAL means 40% off or more against a verified usual price; GOOD DEAL is 20–39% off; ROUTINE SALE is under 20%. Where the same product is on sale at two or more stores, the card flags the cheapest one and the detail page compares them side by side. Prices change fast — confirm the price at checkout before you buy.
